**Decision: `add-alongside`.** The two sets are opposite in polarity, not two instances of one

a single "link policy set" would require an allow/deny table inside the checker, which is exactly
the hand-kept expectation list CLAUDE.md verification rule 2 forbids and which this plan's first
prohibition names. The derived plural set therefore sits alongside the singular constant rather
than absorbing it.

**Accepted debt, and what would force a later promote:** a second INTERNAL link earning disclosure
protection — the live candidate is `about:credits`, recorded as a deliberate not-done in
`deferred-items.md` row 11(a-i). At that point `DISCLOSURE_HREF` becomes a set of two and the
singular constant should be promoted to a derived-or-declared collection, with the decision that
widened it recorded in `deferred-items.md` first, per that row's own stated route.
