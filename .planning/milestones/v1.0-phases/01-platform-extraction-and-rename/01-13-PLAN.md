<user_story>
**As a** person whose Power Browser launch has failed in a way that cannot be retried,
**I want to** see only the actions that will actually do something, and keep the details of what
went wrong,
**so that** I am not sent round a loop that erases the one piece of evidence I could have acted on
or reported.
</user_story>
