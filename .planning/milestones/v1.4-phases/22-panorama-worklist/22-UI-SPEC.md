# Phase 22 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**This is a delta contract.** It adds four Panorama behaviours to the surface that
`.planning/milestones/v1.3-phases/15-panorama-organising/15-UI-SPEC.md` describes (re-pinned in
Phase 21): the Browsing strip follows the active group (W1, W2), a closed group can be restored
(W4), a card closes in place (W5), and the last group cannot be closed (W11, first part).
Everything 15-UI-SPEC and 14-UI-SPEC contract that is not named below stands unchanged. Because
the `gui08` checks derive their expectations from 15-UI-SPEC, every change below lands as an edit
to 15-UI-SPEC in the same commit as the code (see "15-UI-SPEC edits").

Every value was read from the tree at HEAD (`02ce825`, `main`, 2026-10-17):
`tab-strip-widget.tsx` (`mainTabs()`, `homeWidgets()`, `renderTab()`), `chrome-bar-widget.tsx`
(the `+` button, `CHROME_BAR_NEW_TAB_COMMAND_ID`), `group-model.ts` (`load()`, `closeGroup()`,
`setActiveGroup()`, `dissolveGroup()`), `organising-widget.ts` (`closeGroupById()`,
`buildCard()`, `flash()`), `organising-tree.ts`, `panorama-commands.ts`, `modes.css`,
`PowerBrowserAPI.sys.mjs` (`closeGroupRows`), and the checks `verify-gui08-close-exactness.mjs`,
`verify-gui08-canvas-geometry.mjs`, `verify-gui08-panorama-copy.mjs` and
`verify-ng-006-close-group-closes-web-tabs.mjs`.

Sources: 22-CONTEXT.md (Chris's decisions 1 and 2, binding), ROADMAP.md Phase 22 success criteria
1-5, REQUIREMENTS.md PAN-02..PAN-06, CLAUDE.md (hard rules, verification rules, copy rule),
15-UI-SPEC.md (Panorama), 14-UI-SPEC.md (modes and the per-mode strip map), 21-UI-SPEC.md (form),

| Key | Value | Source |

| `.pb-org-card-close` height | 24 px | `modes.css` |

Positions:

- **Card ×**: `position: absolute`, top 4px, right 4px, above the preview, inside every card
  `buildCard()` produces (tiled, loose, stacked front card, expanded-stack card). In a stack and a
  loose pile, only the front card shows it, since only the front card takes the pointer.

  overlaps those bounds, Undo restores the group at its bounds and `pushAway()` runs with the

## Interaction Contract

### Strip follows the active group (W1, PAN-03; D1, D2, A5, A6)

- **Where:** the Browsing tab strip (`TabStripWidget`, very top, above the URL row) only. Coding's
  dock tab bars and Organising are unchanged (D2).
- **What it shows:** with an active group, the strip's own tabs are the home-pane tabs that are
  (a) members of the active group, (b) loose, or (c) the current tab, in the dock's existing order.
  With no active group, or before membership has loaded, or after a failed load, it shows every
  tab, as shipped.
- **A filtered-out tab** is hidden, not closed, moved or detached. It keeps its widget, page and
  dock position, and it does not appear in the home pane's own bar either (it keeps the
  strip-only class). Switching modes still never closes, moves or detaches a tab (14-UI-SPEC
  invariant).
- **The strip updates live**, without a reload, after every membership or activity change: a card
  joins or leaves a group, a group is drawn, created, activated, closed, restored or dissolved,
  "+" opens a tab, a tab closes. Strip and canvas read one membership source (the shared frontend
  reader in `@powerbrowser/modes` beside `GroupModel`); neither keeps its own copy.
- **On launch** with a persisted active group, the strip is filtered from its first paint once
  membership is known, and unfiltered until then. It never paints zero tabs because the model is
  late.
- **Closing the active group** passes activity to a remaining group (as shipped:
  `groups[0]`), and the strip shows that group's tabs plus loose tabs. Dissolving the active
  group leaves no active group, and the strip shows every tab.
- **Empty filtered set:** an active group with no open tabs and no loose tabs shows only the
  current tab. The strip never has a placeholder.

### "+" joins the active group (W2, PAN-03; L1, A7)

- The New Tab command (the `+` button in the URL row, or its keybinding) opens a tab that is a
  member of the active group when one is active, and loose otherwise, in every mode.
- Its card appears at the end of that group's grid in Organising, and its tab appears in the
  Browsing strip. Membership is written through the single chrome-side writer path like every
  other `setTabGroup`.
- If the membership write fails, the tab stays open and loose, and the existing save-error bar
  and Retry apply. No new copy.

### Undo Close Group (W4, PAN-04; A1-A4, A12)

1. × on a box header (or a tree section) closes the group at once. No dialog.
2. The box is removed. Its tabs leave every strip, dock bar and card set (A3). If it was active,
   activity passes to a remaining group. The status-bar flash **"Group "{name}" closed."** shows
   as today.
3. **"Undo Close Group"** appears centred in the group's bounds on the canvas, and in the tree
   in the section's place in group order. Focus moves to it, since the × it came from is gone.
4. Undo (click, or Enter / Space on the focused control) restores the group: same title,
   bounds, membership and card order, its tabs back in place (A3). The restored group is active
   again only if it was active when closed. Focus moves to the restored box title (canvas) or
   section title (tree).
5. After `UNDO_CLOSE_MS` (15000 ms) without Undo, the control fades out and the close commits:
   the tabs close for good as closed-tab history and the group row is removed (today's
   `closeGroupRows` effect, moved from the click to the timer). If focus was on the control, it
   moves to **"New Group"**.
6. Several groups can be in their undo windows at once, each with its own control and timer.
7. Switching mode or view during the window does not end it; the control is still there on
   return while the timer runs. Quit during the window commits the close (A4).
8. Unsaved editor changes are never discarded without Theia's stock save prompt, at the click or
   at the commit.
9. A failed restore or commit write reverts the painted change and raises the existing save-error
   bar with Retry. No new copy.

### Per-card close (W5, PAN-05; A8, A9)

- Every card carries a × (`.pb-org-card-close`), shown on card hover and when the card has focus.
  Click closes that tab. Delete on a focused card does the same.
- The press on × must not start a card drag, and a double click on × must not open the tab
  (stop propagation on `pointerdown` and `dblclick`, as the strip's close does).
- The card leaves its group or the loose line in place, and the grid re-packs. A group emptied
  this way stays and shows **"Empty group — drag tabs here."**
- Focus after a keyboard close: the next card in the same roving scope, else the previous one,
  else the box title (group scope), else **"New Group"** (loose scope). In the expanded-stack
  panel the panel stays open with the remaining cards and closes when none remain, returning
  focus to the box title.
- A card whose tab is not open (a restorable row) closes by marking its row closed; nothing
  reopens.

### The last group stays (W11 first part, PAN-06; A10)

- When exactly one group remains (groups in an undo window not counted), its × is not rendered
  on the canvas box or the tree section. The title widens into the space.
- The close-group command called with that group's id does nothing: no flash, no undo control.
- When a second group appears (New Group, a drawn band, an Undo), both × return.
