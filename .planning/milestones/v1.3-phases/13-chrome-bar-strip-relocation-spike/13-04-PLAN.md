<objective>
Close G-13-3 facets 1 and 3: suggestion activation navigates through a real browser-navigation path, and the dead controls plus stale chip become honest, live UI.

Purpose: the chrome bar shipped in 13-03 looks right but its activation misroutes (opaque row keys into the webview-panel handler, raw text into file-scheme editor opens, failures swallowed to console) and its nav buttons ride the wrong service while the chip goes stale. This plan rewires the commit path onto the proven stock-window channel with a contracted search-or-address mapping, surfaces commit failures in-bar per the copy rules, grounds all three nav controls on one GUI-02-owned predicate, and subscribes the chip to shell events.
Output: fixed widget and commands, rewritten activation gate plus extended copy set, quick green.
</objective>
