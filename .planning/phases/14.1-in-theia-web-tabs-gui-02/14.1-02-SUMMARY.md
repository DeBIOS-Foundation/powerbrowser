## Decisions Made

- **Typing guard = focused AND edited.** The plan's literal guard ("return when the pill input is `document.activeElement`") makes the E3-loading truth unreachable: nothing moves focus on Enter, so after a commit the input stays focused and chrome's canonical URL push (`example.com` -> `https://example.com/`) would never be applied. `syncAddressFromWidget` skips only when the input is focused and `inputValue !== committedAddress` (an uncommitted edit). After a commit both are equal, so the push lands; mid-edit, neither a tab change nor a push overwrites the text.
- **`syncCurrentWidget` helper in the contribution** rather than the plan's inline three-call lambda duplicated for the subscription and the seed.
- **Two-sided stock-window check in the gate**: presence of the web-tab import and absence of `OPEN_BROWSER_WINDOW_COMMAND_ID`, plus an anchor-presence broken-instrument check, so a future rewrite of the routing line cannot leave plants 2 and 4 vacuously green.
- Doc comments in `chrome-bar-commands.ts` do not spell `canGoBack` / `canGoForward` / `canReload` (the acceptance greps require exactly one occurrence each).
