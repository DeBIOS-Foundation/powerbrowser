|----------|-------------|
| Theia frontend (content process, http://127.0.0.1:port) → chrome (`PowerBrowserGroup` actor) | Untrusted DOM events carrying `{ requestId, msg }` cross into `handleGroupMutation` |

| `PowerBrowserAPI` → `tabs.sqlite` | Page URL and title written by the single chrome-side writer |
