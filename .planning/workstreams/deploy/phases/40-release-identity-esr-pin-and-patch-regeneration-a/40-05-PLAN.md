<objective>
Give the allowlist a `user-initiated` disposition (UPD-33) and make the About dialog's
release-notes and manual-update links real pages derived from `[urls]` (UPD-13).

Purpose: links a user clicks (the welcome widget, the About dialog) must be reachable without
widening unattended egress. `user-initiated` names hosts the browser may contact only after a
click. Every endpoint check treats an unattended request to such a host as a failure, exactly
like `deny`.

The About dialog today hides its release-notes link (the pref is unset) and throws on the empty
manual-update URL. After this plan, both come from the manifest. The commit that sets them also
re-dispositions powerbrowser.org and adds github.com as user-initiated.

Output:
- the shared verdict module
- layer 3 rewired onto it, with a fixture self-test row
- the schema accepting the new value
- the link-pref derivation
- the two regenerated branding files
- the allowlist rows
</objective>
