## Decisions Made

- **Positive set equality instead of a log-absence assertion** for G-14.1.1-19, per CLAUDE.md Verification rule 1. The gap's own wording ("logs no TypeError") is an absence claim over Gecko's console; asserted directly it could never be shown to go red. The gate asserts the property that produces the absence, and the emitter is demonstrated separately.
- **`tabs` is a declared, reasoned hold-out.** WebDriver reaches it through a local `tabBrowser` binding in `remote/shared/TabManager.sys.mjs`, so there is no `gBrowser`-receiver call site to derive it from. Rather than fake one, the gate declares it in a one-entry map with its reason, holds it out of the set-equality comparison, and asserts it present separately — a self-test control proves dropping it is still red, so the hold-out is not a blanket exemption.
- **The environment prefix is derived, not spelled.** Reading it out of `powerbrowser-env.ts`'s `startsWith(...)` loop means the Phase 2 rebrand cannot leave the gate quietly matching a prefix nothing uses; an underivable prefix is a distinct broken-instrument failure, and the self-test proves it.
- **`tab-query-service.ts` untouched.** Reserved for another session, and the gate supersedes its comment's job. Its pre-existing unstaged modification was neither staged nor reverted.
