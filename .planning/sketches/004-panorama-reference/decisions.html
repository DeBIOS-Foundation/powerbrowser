A — Keep the tray what we built

A tab with no group lands in a strip along the bottom. Always in the same place, so it is an easy drop target and an easy “where did that go”.

3 · Tabs the store remembers but that are no longer open

A — Hide them Panorama’s answer

The canvas is exactly the open tabs. A closed tab leaves no card. The store keeps membership only so a tab that is still open lands in the right group.

Reading  3 tabs

Simple and honest: what you see is what is open. Nothing to restore from here.

B — Show them as restorable

Closed tabs stay as faded cards in their old group, with a way to bring them back. The group becomes a saved reading list, not just a live view.

More useful, more state to keep honest — and it needs a rule for when a remembered tab is finally forgotten.
